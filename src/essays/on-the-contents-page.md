standfirst: how the index orders itself, and what a filename does.
---

the front page pulls every essay that isn't a draft, sorts newest first,
and types out a table of contents -- title, a row of dots, the year. on a
phone the dots go away and the year drops under the title.

the filename is the url. this file is on-the-contents-page.md, so it lives
at /essays/on-the-contents-page. rename the file and the address changes,
which breaks anything that already pointed at it.
